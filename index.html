Purrwink

20-20-20 eye break reminders with Mochi, for iPhone.

Every 20 minutes, Mochi nudges you to look at something about 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds, and purrs right along with you. Purrwink is free, with no in-app purchases, no ads and no account, and everything stays on your iPhone.
