Frequently asked questions

How do Purrwink reminders work?

Purrwink follows the 20-20-20 habit: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds. Tap Start on the Home tab and Purrwink schedules your reminders. When a break is due, you get a reminder; open it and Mochi purrs with you for 20 seconds while you look away from the screen. Purrwink is here to help you remember to rest your eyes. It isn't medical advice.

What's the difference between Gentle and Relentless?

Gentle sends one soft reminder per break. Relentless reminds you a few more times if you don't start your break, then lets it go until the next break comes around. You can switch anytime in the Settings tab.

My reminders aren't showing up. What can I check?

  1. Make sure your breaks are running: open Purrwink and check that you've tapped Start.
  2. Open iOS Settings › Notifications › Purrwink and make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  3. In the same screen, check that the alert styles you want (Lock Screen, Notification Center, Banners) are on.
  4. If Purrwink is in your Scheduled Summary, reminders may wait for the summary. Turn on Time Sensitive Notifications for Purrwink there so break reminders arrive on time.
  5. If you see a "Notifications off" chip in Purrwink, tap Turn on and follow the steps.

Even with notifications off, Purrwink still reminds you while the app is open.

Time Sensitive isn't showing up, or reminders are quiet during Focus

Purrwink marks break reminders as Time Sensitive so they can reach you on time. iOS lets you control this:

  • For Purrwink: iOS Settings › Notifications › Purrwink › turn on Time Sensitive Notifications.
  • For a Focus: iOS Settings › Focus › choose your Focus (for example Work) › Apps › turn on Time Sensitive Notifications. Or add Purrwink to that Focus's allowed apps.

Psst! If a Focus hushes Time Sensitive notifications, Mochi has to wait until the Focus ends. See Apple's guide, Allow or silence notifications for a Focus on iPhone.

Can I turn Time Sensitive off?

Yes. Turn off Time Sensitive Notifications in iOS Settings › Notifications › Purrwink. Reminders still arrive, but a Focus or Scheduled Summary may hold them.

What are the good-morning and goodnight notes?

Two quiet notes a day, with no sound. Don't want them? Switch them off in the Settings tab.

Can I use Siri or Shortcuts?

Yes. Start a Break opens Purrwink and begins a 20-second break with Mochi right away. Start Session starts your 20-minute reminder cycle, just like tapping Start on Home. There's also Time Until Next Break, and Pause or Resume Reminders. Find them in the Shortcuts app or ask Siri.

What are streaks?

Purrwink shows your streaks and stats in the Streaks tab so you can see your break habit grow, and Mochi cheers you on.

Why does Mochi look sleepy (or dramatic)?

Mochi has moods! Mochi's mood follows your streak and your breaks: happy, content, sleepy, or a little dramatic. Take a break together and see how Mochi feels.

How do I save or share my weekly recap?

Open the Streaks tab, open your weekly recap card, and tap share. In the share sheet, tap Save Image to save it to Photos, or pick where to send it. The card only leaves Purrwink when you choose.

Why does Purrwink ask about Photos?

Only so it can save your recap card when you tap Save Image. iOS asks whether Purrwink may add photos. Purrwink can't see or read your library, and it never uploads anything. If you said no and change your mind, go to iOS Settings › Apps › Purrwink › Photos (on some iOS versions, Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos).

Does Purrwink collect my data? Do I need an account?

No and no. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy (also in the Settings tab › About).

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Purrwink app. That removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Does Purrwink cost anything?

Purrwink is free. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

Which devices does Purrwink support?

Purrwink is designed for iPhone.
