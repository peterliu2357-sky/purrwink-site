Purrwink Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 App: Purrwink for iPhone Developer: Jiaxin Liu

In a nutshellEverything stays on your iPhone: no account, no ads, no tracking, and Purrwink doesn't send your data anywhere. Your weekly recap card leaves the app only if you share it.

The short version: Purrwink doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in Purrwink stays on your iPhone. Your weekly recap card leaves the app only if you share it.

What Purrwink stores, and where

To work, Purrwink keeps a few things on your iPhone only:

  • your settings (for example Gentle or Relentless reminders, and whether the good-morning and goodnight notes are on)
  • your break history, streaks and stats
  • the reminders it has scheduled for you

This information is stored inside the app on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Purrwink doesn't connect to the internet to work, and we have no server that receives your data.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, iOS may include Purrwink's on-device data in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by us.

What Purrwink does not do

  • No account or sign-in, so we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics or usage tracking inside the app.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs. Purrwink is built only with Apple's own system frameworks.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any.

Notifications

Purrwink reminds you to take a break with local notifications. They are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself when you start your breaks. They are not push notifications and they don't pass through any server of ours. If you allow Time Sensitive notifications, reminders can reach you during a Focus. You can turn notifications, Time Sensitive, or the good-morning and goodnight notes off at any time in Purrwink's Settings or in iOS Settings › Notifications › Purrwink. Purrwink still works with notifications off; it reminds you while the app is open.

Photos and sharing

Purrwink makes a weekly recap card, which you can share from the Streaks tab with the standard iOS share sheet.

  • Saving to Photos. Purrwink saves the recap image to your Photos only when you tap Save Image. The first time, iOS asks whether Purrwink may add photos. That is the only Photos permission Purrwink uses.
  • Purrwink can't read your library. Add-only access means Purrwink can't see, open, change or delete any photo you already have.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Saving puts the image into your own photo library on your iPhone. Purrwink never uploads it or anything else to us or anyone.
  • Sharing. If you pick another option in the share sheet (for example Messages or AirDrop), you choose where the card goes, and that app or service handles it under its own terms.

You can change the Photos permission anytime in iOS Settings › Apps › Purrwink › Photos (on some iOS versions, Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos).

Siri and Shortcuts

Purrwink offers Siri and Shortcuts actions (Start a Break, Start Session, Time Until Next Break, Pause or Resume Reminders). These run on your iPhone. When you use Siri, Apple handles your request under Apple's own privacy policy; Purrwink doesn't receive any recordings.

Information Apple may share with developers

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics or crash information with app developers, Apple may give developers aggregated app statistics and crash reports under Apple's own policies. This comes from Apple, not from Purrwink. Purrwink itself doesn't collect or send anything.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your iPhone, you're always in control. To delete all of it, delete the Purrwink app. That removes everything Purrwink stored on the device. (Copies inside your own iPhone backups follow your backup settings.) There's nothing for us to delete, because we never receive it.

Children

Purrwink is meant for a general audience and isn't directed at children. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, of any age, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If Purrwink ever started collecting any data, we would update this policy and Purrwink's App Store privacy details before that change took effect.
